## Rule

A marketplace seller **may mark items in their own sale as non-returnable**. That designation
is set and owned by the seller at listing level, independent of Company A's common returns
policy.

Responsibility is split by function, not by who touches the customer:

- **The seller owns the sale** — pricing, sale terms, and any seller-set exclusions such as
  "sale items are non-returnable."
- **Company A owns fulfilment** — an order may be sold by a marketplace seller and still be
  fulfilled from a Company A warehouse. Fulfilment-side obligations (delivery, warehouse
  handling, the common returns policy) attach to Company A regardless of who sold the item.

## Scope

Applies to any order where the listing party (marketplace seller) differs from the fulfilling
party (Company A warehouse). It does not apply to items both sold and fulfilled directly by
Company A.

## Related

- A seller's non-returnable designation can conflict with Company A's defect-return rule in
  `KB-RET-01` (for example, a defective sale item). That conflict is **not resolved here**;
  precedence between the two is undefined and is tracked in `KB-PRE-01`.
- Warranty is honoured by Company A regardless of seller: `KB-WAR-01`.
