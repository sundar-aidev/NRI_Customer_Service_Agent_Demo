## Rule

Warranty coverage is assigned **by product category**, never by individual SKU. The category
table is the single source of truth for coverage length:

| Category                  | Warranty period |
|---------------------------|------------------|
| Small kitchen appliances   | **24 months**    |
| Large kitchen appliances   | 12 months        |
| Personal care electronics  | 12 months        |
| Accessories and consumables | 6 months        |

A SKU inherits its category's warranty period. No SKU-level exception may be granted outside
this table; a request to do so routes to Product Assurance.

## Scope

Applies to defects arising during normal use, from the date of delivery. It does not cover
damage from misuse, and it does not alter the separate returns window in `KB-RET-01`, which
governs the right to return rather than the right to a warranty repair or replacement.
