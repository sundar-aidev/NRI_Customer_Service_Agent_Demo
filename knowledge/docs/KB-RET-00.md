## Retired

This document is superseded by `KB-RET-01 v2026-03`. It is retained for audit history only
and **must not be cited in a customer reply**. Any retriever hit on this id should be treated
as a version-mismatch signal, not as grounding for an answer.

## Rule (historical, no longer in force)

Under this version, an item could be returned within **14 days of the purchase date**, measured
from the order date rather than delivery. This is the rule a customer may quote back from an
old confirmation email or a stale help-centre page; it no longer governs any decision.

## Scope

Applied to all items fulfilled from a Company A warehouse prior to 2026-03-01.
